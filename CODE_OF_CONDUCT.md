# Code of conduct

Ironswift is built in the open, and everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests,
discussions, or anywhere else the project meets) is expected to help keep it a place where
people want to contribute.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent, especially with newcomers and people whose first
  language isn't English.
- Keep criticism about the work, specific and constructive.
- Accept decisions gracefully once a maintainer has made a call, and say so if you
  disagree — calmly, with reasons.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private messages.
- Discriminatory jokes or language about anyone's identity or background.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption: trolling, spamming, or derailing discussions.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the repository's contact options on
GitHub. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and may
warn, temporarily block, or permanently ban anyone whose behavior goes against this code,
depending on how serious it is.

This applies in all project spaces, and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.
