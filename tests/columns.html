The Galveston County Historical Commission met on Tuesday to review the restoration plans for the old interurban depot. Members heard from architects who described how the original brick could be cleaned and repointed without damaging the surface.

Volunteers have catalogued more than four hundred photographs from the collection, many showing the bayou before the 1915 storm. Each image is scanned, labeled with the date and place when known, and stored with notes from the families who donated it.

The commission also approved a marker for the site of the first schoolhouse. The text will be finished this winter and the dedication is planned for the spring, with students from the nearby elementary school taking part in the ceremony.
