Floats

Pull quote. This box floats right; text from both sides flows between the two floats.

Text wraps around floated content the way newspapers and older websites lay it out. This paragraph starts beside a picture floated left and a box floated right, so each line is narrowed by both floats until it passes their bottoms. After that, lines use the full width of the column again, which you can see once the text runs long enough to get past the taller of the two floated boxes. More words make the effect easier to see: the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog, again and again, until the floats end.

This paragraph has clear: both, so it starts below both floats.
